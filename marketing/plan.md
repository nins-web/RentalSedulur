## 1. Executive Summary
UMKM rental PS 8 unit, USP paket malam hemat 40% + anti double-booking. Target 20 booking/bulan dalam 90 hari, 60% dari organik Google + 30% WA referral + 10% Instagram. Budget <500k/bulan, tim 1-2 orang.

## 2. Current State Audit (0-5)
Acquisition 2/5 (hanya WA manual), Activation 3/5 (katalog bagus tapi SEO 0), Retention 1/5 (belum ada CRM), Referral 1/5, Revenue 2/5, Analytics 1/5, SEO 1/5, Offers 3/5

## 3-7. AARRR

### Acquisition (Bulan 1-3)
- **Programmatic SEO**: 15 halaman lokasi `rental-ps-[kota]` → Mojokerto, Sooko, Puri, Jetis, Sidoarjo, Jombang, Surabaya Barat (template + data unit real)
- **Google Business Profile**: verifikasi, foto 8 unit real, jam 09-23, kategori "Video game rental", posting mingguan paket malam
- **Social**: TikTok/IG Reels 2x/minggu — mabar malam + nobar bola, CTA WA 6281289538855, hashtag #rentalpsmojokerto
- **Partnership**: warung kopi, rental sound, komunitas bola/futsal

### Activation
- Landing sudah bagus (dark hero + light booking). Tambah: badge "Tersedia hari ini" live, hitung total otomatis, toast bentrok + suggest unit lain (sudah ada trigger DB)

### Retention
- WA broadcast list (manual dulu): reminder 7 hari setelah sewa "Mau booking lagi diskon 10k?", pakai Mautic nanti jika 50+ kontak
- Google Sheet CRM sederhana: nama, WA, tanggal, unit, status

### Referral
- Foto testimoni + "Tag IG @rentalsedulur dapat potongan 10k next booking"
- QR di unit: "Scan untuk review Google" → naik rating GBP

### Revenue
- **Offer**: paket malam pusatkan, bundle "PS4 + 2 stik extra + snack 500k/minggu" (value stack), garansi "Stik rusak ganti free"
- Pricing: pertahankan 130k/75k/500k, test paket "Weekend 2 hari 200k" bulan 2
